About

I am a mathematics researcher working in the areas of coding theory, error-correcting codes, sequence design, and related areas of discrete mathematics.

My research interests include the mathematical foundations of reliable communication, combinatorial constructions, and the design and analysis of codes and sequences.

Research

Coding Theory

Mathematical theory and construction of error-correcting codes.

Error-Correcting Codes

Codes for reliable communication over noisy and deletion channels.

Sequence Design

Design and analysis of sequences with desirable correlation and communication properties.

Combinatorics

Combinatorial methods and discrete mathematical structures arising in coding theory.
