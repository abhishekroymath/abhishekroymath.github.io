Dr. Abhishek Roy

Postdoctoral Researcher

Department of Electronics and Electrical Engineering
Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati

My research interests lie in discrete mathematics, combinatorial constructions, and their applications to wireless communications.

I am particularly interested in the design of sequences and code sets with desirable correlation properties, error-correcting codes, and related problems in information and communication theory.

Research Interests

Coding Theory

Error-correcting codes, deletion-correcting codes, and related combinatorial constructions.

Sequence Design

Complementary sequences, zero-correlation-zone sequences, code sets, and correlation properties.

Wireless Communications

Sequence and code design for multi-carrier communication systems, including applications to wireless communication.
