Research

My research focuses on problems in coding theory, sequence design, combinatorial constructions, and their applications to wireless communications.

Research Interests

Coding Theory

My interests include error-correcting codes, deletion-correcting codes, and combinatorial constructions for reliable communication.

Sequence Design

I work on the construction and analysis of sequences with desirable correlation properties, including complementary sequences and zero-correlation-zone sequences.

Code Sets and Array Codes

My research also includes code sets, two-dimensional array codes, and related combinatorial structures motivated by communication systems.

Wireless Communications

I am interested in applications of sequence and code design to wireless communication systems, including multi-carrier communication and related communication technologies.
